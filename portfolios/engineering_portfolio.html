Featured Project

Research · Forecasting · AI

Comparative Analysis of ML & DL Models for Short-Term Electricity Load Forecasting

A comprehensive ML/DL research project benchmarking models for short-term electricity demand forecasting using high-resolution data from Delhi, India. Compared Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, Ensemble Learning, and LSTM with weather and temporal features. Demonstrated that well-engineered classical ML models with advanced feature engineering — lag features, rolling statistics, cyclical temporal encoding, and Optuna-based hyperparameter optimization — can outperform computationally expensive deep learning approaches on structured tabular data.
